## Visual brief

Elderly hands folded over a worn Bible or rosary — hands only, no face. Soft window light. Warm neutral tones. The kind of image that looks like it could be your grandmother's.

Alternative: an empty kitchen chair pulled slightly away from a small table, a mug and open book on the table, morning light through curtains.

Portrait 4:5.

## Caption

I see her at 5:15 every morning.

She kneels by the same chair. She names each of her grandchildren. She doesn't rush.

She thinks I don't notice. I notice.

Wherever you are today — whether you pray like her or you are still learning how to begin — I see you too.

"The prayer of a righteous person has great power as it is working."
— James 5:16
