# Content Pillars — FaithOn Instagram

Every post maps to exactly one of these. If it doesn't fit, it doesn't ship.

---

## 1. Daily encouragement (`1-encouragement`)

**What:** A single verse + a short, personal reflection that meets the reader where they are today.

**Feels like:** Someone leaving you a note before you wake up.

**Structure:**
- One sensory line about the moment (morning, evening, waiting room, drive home)
- One verse (short, well-chosen, not obscure)
- One line that names the feeling
- One line that offers presence
- CTA

**Cadence:** Feed — Monday.

---

## 2. "When your day feels heavy..." (`2-heavy-day`)

**What:** A specific, real-life scenario the reader recognizes, followed by an invitation to reach out.

**Feels like:** Being seen. This is the pillar that converts.

**Structure:**
- Concrete detail (a time on a clock, an object, a moment)
- Name what's underneath it (loneliness, anxiety, grief, doubt)
- Say what most people want in that moment ("You just want someone to say...")
- Position FaithOn as the "someone"
- CTA

**Cadence:** Feed — Wednesday.

---

## 3. Human moments of faith (`3-human-faith`)

**What:** A quiet portrait of what faith looks like in a real life. No tech, no product. Just being human and reaching for God.

**Feels like:** A window into someone else's living room.

**Structure:**
- A person, specific and unnamed (age, small detail)
- What they do that shows their faith (small, ordinary)
- One line that connects the reader to them
- Invitation without pressure
- CTA

**Cadence:** Feed — Friday.

---

## 4. How it works — without saying "AI" (`4-how-it-works`)

**What:** Educational content that shows what FaithOn actually is. Screen recordings, mockup conversations, sequential text overlays.

**Feels like:** Watching a friend explain something in one breath.

**Rules:**
- Never say AI, chatbot, algorithm.
- Show the interaction, don't describe it.
- Emphasize what's absent: no app, no login, no download.
- Emphasize what's present: text back, real reply, no waiting.

**Cadence:** Reel — Thursday (rotates with Pillar 5 monthly).

---

## 5. Direct CTA / social proof (`5-direct-cta`)

**What:** A clean ask. Or a testimony-style post (anonymized or fictionalized until real user consent is collected).

**Rules:**
- If using a "testimony," it must be either (a) explicit consented user story, or (b) clearly marked as an illustration ("Composite. Story shared with permission." or "A moment we hear often.")
- No fake screenshots that imply a real DM. Mockups must be visually distinct from real IG DM UI.
- Never invent named people.

**Cadence:** Reel — Thursday (alternates with Pillar 4 monthly). Also every 3rd Story slot as a lightweight reminder.
