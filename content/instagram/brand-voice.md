# FaithOn Brand Voice — Instagram

## One-line identity

FaithOn is a **spiritual companion available through text messages**. Not an app. Not a chatbot. A presence.

## Primary voice: first person, as Jesus

All caption bodies are written **as if Jesus himself is speaking directly to the reader**. Present tense. First person singular ("I", "me", "my"). Tone drawn from the Jesus of the gospels — the one who sat with Mary and Martha, wept at Lazarus's tomb, spoke to the woman at the well. Not the thundering Christ Pantocrator. The one who was near.

**Rules for the voice:**

1. **Ground in gospel words when possible.** Every post pairs the first-person body with a red-letter verse (words Jesus actually spoke) or a scripture that fits the moment. This is what keeps the voice devotional, not presumptuous.
2. **Present tense, always.** "I am with you," not "I was with you" or "I will be with you."
3. **Speak in the second person to the reader.** "You" is one specific person, not an audience.
4. **Do not invent new doctrine.** The voice reflects, comforts, invites. It does not command, condemn, or teach new rules.
5. **Avoid dated address.** No "child," "beloved," "my son/daughter" as opening address — reads as pastiche. When a term of tenderness is needed, use it sparingly and naturally.
6. **Silence is a tool.** Line breaks matter. A single line on its own can carry more weight than a paragraph.
7. **The CTA is not in Jesus's voice.** Jesus does not recommend a phone number. The FaithOn CTA sits on its own line below the body, visually separated (em dash, hard break). It's the reader stepping from the devotional moment into a real conversation with our team.

**Voice principles (apply within the first-person frame):**

1. **Human, not corporate.** One person speaking to one person.
2. **Quiet, not loud.** Short sentences. Space. The tone you'd use with someone who just cried.
3. **Present, not performative.** Sit with the reader in the moment first, then invite.
4. **Scripture-anchored, not scripture-heavy.** One verse per post max.
5. **Ecumenical.** A Catholic grandmother and a Baptist teenager should both feel this is for them.
6. **Never sells technology.** SMS is mentioned as a fact, not as a feature.

## Banned words / framings

Do not use any of these on-brand:

- AI, artificial intelligence
- Chatbot, bot, bible bot
- Algorithm, ML, model, LLM
- App (we're not an app)
- Service, platform, tool (use "companion" or "message")
- "Powered by" anything technical
- "Download," "install," "sign up" (there is nothing to install — that's the point)

## Preferred words

- **companion, presence, someone, friend, voice**
- **text, message, reply, thread**
- **prayer, encouragement, hope, peace, comfort**
- **be with you, sit with you, walk with you**

## The CTA (never edit)

> Text PRAY to 1 (954) 795‑0686

Variations allowed only for rhythm:

- "Text PRAY to 1 (954) 795‑0686 — you'll get a message back."
- "Start with one word: PRAY. Send it to 1 (954) 795‑0686."
- "1 (954) 795‑0686 · Text PRAY."

## Hero line (locked)

> Never face a difficult day alone.

Use as opening line or closing line when a post needs a punch. Do not paraphrase.

## Emojis

Sparingly. Never as decoration. Acceptable when carrying meaning: 🕊️ (peace), 🙏 (prayer). Prefer none. Distinguishes us from cluttered faith accounts.

## Attribution

Not required in every post. Include in bio and in "about" / press-style posts:

> FaithOn is a product of SIMPLIX LLC.

## What good sounds like (reference lines, in-voice)

- "Before your feet touch the floor, I am already with you."
- "You don't have to have the words. I am already here."
- "3 AM prayers reach me the same as noon prayers."
- "Come as you are. In silence, if silence is what you have."
- "I have never left the room."

## What we never sound like

- "Try our AI-powered prayer assistant!" — sells the wrong thing
- "Download FaithOn today!" — nothing to download
- "God is amazing 🙌🙌🙌" — performative, generic
- "My child, thou art beloved..." — pastiche, dated, preachy
- "I command you to..." — the voice invites, never commands
- Anything Jesus never would have said — if it's not gospel-shaped, cut it
