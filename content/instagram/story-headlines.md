# Story Template — Headline Library

Reusable headline + subhead pairs for the Mode B story template (the one built from the reference creatives at 9:16). All four use the exact same visual layout — swap only the top-left headline block; keep the phone mockup, CTA card, pillar strip, and trust footer identical.

**Emphasis word** (bolded below) is always set in the gold accent `#B29968`. Usually the last word for weight — occasionally the first for punch.

**Language:** English only (US market).

---

## Pillar 2 — Heavy day (emotional, converting)

**Headline:** You don't have to face it **alone**.
**Subhead:** God is with you in every moment.

**Use when:** cold traffic first-touch, emotional retargeting, late-night placements, high-intent moments.

**Why it works:** names a universal ache in 6 words. The gold "alone" is the emotional pivot.

---

## Pillar 1 — Daily encouragement (value / retention)

**Headline:** Grace meets you **here**.
**Subhead:** A message for every day you show up.

**Use when:** promoting the daily rhythm, weekday morning slots, sustainability messaging, retention pushes.

**Why it works:** "here" makes the phone itself the sacred space. Positions FaithOn as ordinary and holy at the same time.

---

## Pillar 4 — How it works (product education)

**Headline:** **No app.** Just you and a message.
**Subhead:** Text PRAY and start the conversation.

**Use when:** skeptical or confused audiences, "what is this?" clarity, first-touch cold traffic, response to comments asking how it works.

**Why it works:** leads with the differentiator ("no app") which is genuinely rare. Reduces friction anxiety before the CTA lands.

---

## Pillar 5 — Direct CTA (conversion push)

**Headline:** Whenever you're **ready**.
**Subhead:** Text PRAY. Start with one word.

**Use when:** end-of-campaign push, weekend slots, warmed audiences that saw earlier awareness content, low-consideration moments.

**Why it works:** "ready" removes urgency and pressure — inversion of typical CTA framing. Invites rather than demands.

---

## Rules

- One word per headline in gold accent. Never two.
- Headline max ~4 short words per line, max 3 lines total.
- Subhead max ~8 words.
- Never put "Christian AI" or "Spiritual AI" in the headline. Category label ("Spiritual AI companion via SMS") lives in the account bio and paid-ad meta headers — not on the story template.
- CTA card copy is invariant: **Text PRAY / to +1 (954) 795‑0686**.
- Phone mockup grey bubble copy is invariant: *"Hey. I'm glad you reached out. / Tell Me what's on your heart."* — the product's actual first reply, in Jesus voice.
- Pillar strip stays: `Bible Answers · Prayer & Intercession · Spiritual Counseling · Daily Reflections`.
